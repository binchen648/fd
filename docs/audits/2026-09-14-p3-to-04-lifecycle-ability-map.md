## Mapping Invariants

- The 11 rows are the corrected explicit lifecycle/reset denominator; modifier-local lifecycle fields outside this ability-level axis do not silently enlarge the denominator.
- Card and ability IDs identify evidence rows only. Generic runtime eligibility must be based on normalized lifecycle shape, never named-card branches.
- Usage counters are server-owned. Prompt creation, declined optional choices, cancelled interactions, stale commands, and failed effect transactions do not consume a usage unless an independently reviewed policy explicitly defines an earlier committed reservation boundary.
- Per-game `this_card` state follows the same authoritative card instance/ability scope across ordinary zone moves and reconnect; an unrelated transformed definition/ability cannot inherit the record without explicit reviewed transfer semantics.
- Unique-group arbitration is keyed by controller + semantic `groupId` + authoritative trigger-window identity. Trigger ordering and player choice remain external Trigger/Interaction responsibilities.
- `when_card_leaves_active_area` cleans lifecycle-owned state after an accepted source transition. It does not move/close the card itself.
- `remain_active` forbids Lifecycle from performing automatic round-end movement solely due cleanup; it does not prevent another accepted rule from closing/removing the source.
- `expire_after_duration` requires a resolved cleanup destination policy before runtime admission. If authoring plus accepted card-zone rules do not determine a destination, migration remains blocked rather than guessing from card names.
