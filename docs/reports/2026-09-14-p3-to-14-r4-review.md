## [P1] Base VP source schema still has a catch-all and omits battle-phase recon identity

The canonical schema currently allows:

`source: 'battle_vp' | 'event_vp' | 'competition_vp' | 'reviewed_rule'`.

This is unsafe for two reasons.

1. `reviewed_rule` is an open semantic catch-all. A later implementation could route a card-specific personal win reward back into the base scoring plan and silently bypass the newly accepted post-base-scoring barrier.
2. Canonical rules grant every legal player at Recon +2 VP **at battle-power-resolution start**, once. Golden Flow 2 explicitly treats recon +2 VP as part of the representative scoring flow. Current runtime also distinguishes `location_vp` and `recon_vp`, while the TO-14 canonical source union contains neither a closed location-reward source nor a phase-level recon reward identity.

Required repair:

- remove generic `reviewed_rule` from base scoring source types;
- use a closed discriminated union for base battlefield reward sources, at minimum event-pool share and competition-pool share, plus an explicit typed location reward only when a reviewed location rule supplies it;
- model Recon +2 VP as a separate battle-phase resource plan/receipt committed once at battle-power-resolution start, not as a per-battlefield winner adjustment;
- give recon plan/receipt its own exactly-once identity and stale/reconnect guarantees;
- keep card/master/servant personal rewards outside both base battlefield scoring and recon plan; they remain typed Resource settlements after the base barrier at their authored timing;
- update fail-closed/checklist/result text so a generic source cannot reappear.

No denominator change is required.
