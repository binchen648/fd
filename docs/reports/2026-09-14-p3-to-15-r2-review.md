## Prior Blocker Closure

### Typed scope/value contract — CLOSED

The canonical modifier contract no longer carries arbitrary `unknown` constraints or values. It uses normalized subject/object/constraint/applicability contracts and finite literal or compiled numeric-expression values with declared authoritative input-policy references.

Unsupported normalized payloads fail before runtime storage.

### Authoritative trace versus projection — CLOSED

Authoritative `PowerTraceLine` retains real provenance and references a `projectionPolicyId`. Viewer projection is a separate discriminated representation:

- `none` retains source and details;
- `source_identity` omits source identity while preserving permitted calculation detail;
- `details` structurally cannot carry source/input/operand/output detail.

Reconnect/projection therefore cannot replace authoritative trace with a redacted copy.

### RULE/EFFECT modifier ownership — CLOSED

The canonical contract is now discriminated:

- `RuleModifierContract` directly owns its reviewed `operation + ruleKey` semantics;
- `EffectModifierContract` owns a typed `effectPolicyId` and emits zero or more normalized `ModifierContribution` records;
- each contribution has a mandatory typed `target` (`rule` or `power_layer`) and its own operation/scope/value/priority validation;
- ability-level `EFFECT_MODIFIER` does not authorize Modifier/Power to absorb movement, random discard, VP transfer, Card Zone mutation, status creation, branching, or other non-modifier effects.

This matches the actual 9 effect-modifier shapes, including mixed-effect abilities.
