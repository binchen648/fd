### [P1] TO-03 minimum battle identity is not explicit in the revised event payloads

Accepted Trigger Gateway says later runtime specs may extend payloads but may not drop required identity. Its battle events require battle identity; the phase-terminal event requires battle identity/participant set/terminal state. TO-14 currently says `phase/result identity` and for `after_battle_ended` phase identity + result IDs, but does not explicitly carry the underlying `battleId`/ordered `battleIds`.

Repair: every per-battlefield event payload must explicitly carry `battlePhaseResolutionId + battleId + resultId`. Phase-terminal `after_battle_ended` must carry `battlePhaseResolutionId`, ordered resolved `battleIds`, ordered `resultIds`, scoring receipt IDs, aggregate participant references, and terminal state.

### [P1] Candidate invents a relative order for gains-victory vs other post-battle triggers

Stage 6 settles result/win/loss/first-loss triggers, then Stage 7 produces/settles `after_controller_gains_victory`. Canonical rules group post-battle / won / lost effects but do not establish this global relative order. TO-03 already requires explicit orderingRef and blocks unresolved semantic collisions.

Repair: once all base scoring receipts exist, derive any gains-victory event identities and place them in the **same phase-wide post-battle trigger set** as result/win/loss/first-loss events. Trigger Gateway owns their relative ordering. If ordering can change outcome and no reviewed rule resolves it, keep that collision blocked. Do not hardcode gains-victory after the other four event families.

Also make first-loss production explicitly require an authoritative reviewed loss ordinal; simultaneous multi-battlefield loss with unresolved ordinal must remain blocked rather than guessed.
