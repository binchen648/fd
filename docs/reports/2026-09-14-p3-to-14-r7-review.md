## Blocking finding

### [P1] Post-battle barrier is scoped per battlefield instead of across the whole battle-power-resolution phase

Canonical rules require the order:

1. Recon reward at power-resolution start.
2. Resolve and score all battlefields.
3. Then resolve “after battle phase / after battle / if you won or lost” effects.
4. Then cleanup.

The candidate instead says that after one battlefield's base scoring receipt commits, its `post_base_scoring` barrier may open and its result/win/loss/first-loss triggers may settle immediately. In a two-battlefield round this permits Miyama post-battle effects to mutate VP/resources/state before Shinto base scoring is committed.

That order is not supported by `docs/rules/FD-Game-Rules-Final.md` lines 691-694 and the round-end sequence at lines 642-651. The two current `after_battle_ended` consumers also have printed clauses meaning battle-phase end (`战斗阶段结束时/后`), not “one battlefield finished”.

Required repair:

- replace the per-battlefield post-base barrier with one server-owned battle-phase barrier keyed by `battlePhaseResolutionId`;
- determine and base-score every supported battlefield before opening ordinary result/win/loss/first-loss trigger settlement;
- if any battlefield is blocked before base scoring, do not open the phase-wide post-battle barrier for a partially scored phase unless a future reviewed rule explicitly defines that recovery;
- after all result/win/loss/first-loss/gains-victory consumers are terminal, emit one phase-terminal `after_battle_ended` event carrying phase identity plus the resolved battle/result identities required by consumers;
- only then hand off to cleanup/lifecycle;
- do not treat `after_battle_ended` as a per-battlefield event.
