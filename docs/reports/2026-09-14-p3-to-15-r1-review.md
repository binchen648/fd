## Blocking Finding

### [P1] A single required `operation + ruleKey` contract incorrectly collapses EFFECT_MODIFIER semantics into RULE_MODIFIER semantics

The canonical type still requires every modifier to have:

```ts
axis: ModifierAxis;
operation: ModifierOperation;
ruleKey: string;
```

where `ModifierOperation` is `ignore | forbid | set | add | multiply`.

That fits normalized rule/power contributions, but the current 9 `EFFECT_MODIFIER` rows include semantic effects such as:

- `create_status`;
- `reverse_situation_and_event_power_modifiers`;
- `opponents_random_discard` combined with `set_opponent_power_to_zero`;
- `terrain_multiplier` plus a separate VP transfer;
- branch/move-card plus `create_modifier(power_bonus)`.

An ability-level `EFFECT_MODIFIER` classification does not mean the entire effect can or should be converted into one numeric/rule operation. For example, `create_status` is a typed persistent status producer whose later consequences may affect rules/power; `reverse_situation_and_event_power_modifiers` is a source-class transformation; and a branch can contain both movement and a power contribution.

Requiring one generic `operation + ruleKey` for the whole effect modifier would either:

- invent fake rule keys/operations;
- lose non-modifier side effects;
- or create another catch-all effect interpreter under the Modifier owner.

All three violate the stated ownership split.

**Required repair:** make the canonical contract discriminated by axis. A RULE modifier may own `operation + ruleKey + scope`. An EFFECT modifier must retain a typed `effectPolicyId`/effect-state contract and may optionally expose one or more normalized modifier/power contributions, each with its own operation/rule/layer/value/source identity. Non-modifier side effects stay with their typed effect owners. The ability-level EFFECT_MODIFIER label must not authorize Modifier/Power to absorb the entire ability effect list.
