## Blocking finding

### [P1] Winner eligibility exclusion is incorrectly modeled as mutually exclusive with losing the battle

The candidate schema currently defines a participant outcome as one of:

- winner
- loser
- excluded

It then asks Battle Result to produce a loser set "separately from excluded participants." This conflates two different rule dimensions.

Canonical battle rules state:

- a player with 【败北】 is completely ignored **when determining the highest legal winner** and cannot win;
- a player who does **not participate** in the battle-power resolution is neither considered to have won nor lost;
- 【败北】 remains present during post-battle "if you won/lost" processing.

Current resolver behavior is consistent with eligibility and loss being separable: winner selection filters cannot-win participants, while military/loss handling operates over non-winner participants, with separate suppression for loss effects.

Therefore a participating player may simultaneously be:

- ineligible to win because of a typed exclusion; **and**
- a battle loser for outcome/history/trigger purposes; **and**
- independently immune to some loss effects.

Required repair:

1. Replace the mutually-exclusive `winner | loser | excluded` participant outcome with orthogonal fields/contracts:
   - participation;
   - win eligibility + typed exclusion reason/policy;
   - battle outcome (winner/loser, or explicit nonparticipant if the envelope intentionally includes nonparticipants);
   - loss-effect eligibility/suppression.
2. `loserPlayerIds` must be derived from participating non-winners according to the reviewed rules, not by subtracting `excludedPlayerIds`.
3. Keep `excludedPlayerIds` as winner-eligibility audit information, not an alternative outcome class.
4. Update fail-closed checks so they validate the orthogonal invariants instead of forbidding a player from being both excluded-from-winning and a loser.
5. Add reviewer checklist coverage for defeated/cannot-win participant vs true nonparticipant.
