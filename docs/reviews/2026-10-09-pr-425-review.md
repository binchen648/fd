attack to dissolve when closed or at round end, not enter discard or game-out,

The new cleanup branch at interpreter.ts:1418-1430 moves it to removed_from_game.

observes the same physical instance still in state.cards with that zone.

This is also a dispatch-contract conflict: the base dispatch calls for removal
on duration expiry, and the implementer tests explicitly expect removed_from_game.
Passing those tests does not resolve the higher-priority Final rules requirement.
Do not silently alter either authority during review. Planner/rule owner must
reconcile the contract, and an authorized implementer must then supply a new
candidate and tests covering actual dissolution and no physical-card accounting.
This review does not prescribe a particular internal tombstone representation.
