   dispatch accepts loans of both to different opponents. At one battlefield,

   the physical-card array yields p1=p2=p3=10,p4=8, winners p1/p2/p3.
   The first result no longer satisfies the first Guard's equal-power clause.
   Resolve overlapping links consistently and test both legal simultaneous
   loans/orders; do not silently invent a one-loan restriction absent authority.
